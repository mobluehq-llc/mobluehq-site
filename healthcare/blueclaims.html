blueClaims

Claims shadow-review, health and P&C lanes

Anchors a proposed claims-review packet to the exact policy clauses and record evidence that earned it, and types every “not found” so a denial can never claim absence of evidence the review couldn't have found anyway.

Flagship — demo-ready
Request a pilot conversation Read the manual
Runs locally · CLI or web UI · Ed25519-signed receipts · no cloud account required
Who it's for

Claims adjusters and compliance leads at a payer or carrier.

What it is not

blueClaims never approves, denies, pays, reserves, or alters a claim. The human adjuster is the only adjudication authority.

How it works
1
Load a claim, its policy, and its supporting records — synthetic in the demo, real files in a pilot.
2
The engine maps every clause to its evidence and surfaces contradictions and missing evidence.
3
Each gap is typed SEARCHED-ABSENT, NEVER-SEARCHED, or UNDETECTABLE-WITH-THESE-RECORDS — never a single undifferentiated “not found.”
4
The adjuster reviews the packet in the Workbench, reaches a decision, and exports it in one of five formats.
5
The signed packet is independently verifiable offline.
Status, measured

Engine, receipts, persistence, CLI, API, and a working web UI are real and tested for both lanes (health and property & casualty). All five export formats, run ancestry, interrupted-run recovery, drafts/autosave, seeded-scale performance testing, an accessibility audit, and clean-machine packaging are done.

Not yet true: Library pagination at large record counts is a known, named gap. No signed pilot. No live PHI.

A signed receipt proves what blueClaims ran and recorded; it does not prove an underlying real-world claim is true. blueClaims does not approve, deny, recommend, pay, retract, or alter anything a named human is responsible for deciding.
