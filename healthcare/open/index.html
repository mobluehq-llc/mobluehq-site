blueOpenHealthcare

The receipt spine, verifier, record core and jurisdiction interfaces of blueHealthcare are going to be open, so that health systems that cannot pay for software can still run a signed, verifiable clinical record — and so that the people who know their country's coding, privacy and language can teach us. The licence and contribution terms are being decided by counsel; code contributions open when they are published. Until then, this is a place to read the interfaces, tell us what your jurisdiction needs, and argue with the design. No patient data, ever — real or "anonymised" — anywhere in this community.
