Governance

Roles, moderation, and credit

Open — homeWhyArchitectureSpecContributeFixturesGovernanceSecurityRoadmapDownloadsCommunity

Nothing below describes infrastructure that exists yet — no public repository, forum, or issue tracker is live today. This page states the intended shape, so it can be checked against what actually ships.

Intended roles
  • Maintainer — the company, and only once a maintainer is funded. A community edition is a commitment to build, not a release date.
  • Country pack steward — one named, in-country person per jurisdiction pack.
  • Reviewer — clinical, privacy, or terminology, labelled by what they review.
  • Contributor — anyone filing a field report, a glossary entry, or, later, a pack.
Moderation

A short code of conduct plus the PHI rule (see fixtures), a named moderator, and a takedown path for anything that looks like real patient data: remove it, purge it from history, notify the person who posted it, and record that it happened — without the data itself — in a public digest.

Recognition

Credit is the name on the pack, and a contributors list once there's a repository to hold one. No swag programme; no leaderboard.

Decisions, in public

The intent is that pack and interface changes go through a written proposal the company answers in public — the same thread doubling as the "what we learned" record described on the roadmap page.

Stewardship

MOBLUEHQ, Inc. runs the programme today. It may move to a non-profit foundation later, once there are real deployments or a funder to justify the move — many global-health funders and ministries of health trust a neutral steward more than a vendor, the way OpenMRS is run by a non-profit and DHIS2 by a university programme.

Contributor terms are written now so that rights can pass to that foundation later without renegotiating every contribution.

The licence and contribution terms are being decided by counsel; code contributions open when they are published. See the eligibility and share-back proposal.
