The record that defends the decision

Healthcare decisions are getting easier to automate and harder to defend in an appeal or an audit. blueHealthcare produces the missing artifact: a signed receipt of the process, verifiable by anyone, anywhere, with no account and no network. An overlay on the systems you already run. A named human still decides.

Change one byte, the receipt fails

Every product signs its output with Ed25519 and ships a standalone, dependency-light verifier that checks the signature with no server, no database, and no network connection. Tamper a single byte of a signed packet and verification fails — a real, run tamper battery, not a marketing claim. A signed receipt proves what the software ran and recorded; it never proves an underlying real-world claim is true.
