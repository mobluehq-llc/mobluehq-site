blueChart

Utilization-management evidence mapper

Maps a coverage requirement to the exact chart evidence that supports, contradicts, or never addresses it — then hands a human medical director a signed packet, not a verdict.

Flagship — demo-ready
Request a pilot conversation Read the manual
Runs locally · CLI or web UI · Ed25519-signed receipts · no cloud account required
Who it's for

Medical directors and UM reviewers at a health plan or delegated UM vendor.

What it is not

blueChart never approves, denies, recommends, or alters coverage. It is not a prior-authorization decision engine.

How it works
1
Load the coverage requirement (a policy or criteria set) and the member's chart evidence.
2
blueChart's deterministic matcher runs the evidence map: every requirement gets one of five typed lanes — SUPPORTED, CONTRADICTED, ABSENT, UNVERIFIABLE, or CANNOT-CERTIFY.
3
A reviewer opens the packet, inspects each lane's linked evidence, and reaches their own determination.
4
The packet is signed and exported for the case file.
5
Anyone can verify the signature offline, without installing blueChart, with the standalone verifier.
