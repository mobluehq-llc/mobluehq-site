What a pack for the United Kingdom would carry
Pack fieldWhat we'd expect, to be validated in-country
coding_systemICD-10 5th edition (diagnoses) and OPCS-4 (procedures) in place of the US pack's ICD-10-CM/CPT.
privacy_regimeA UK Data Protection Act 2018, UK GDPR, and NHS data-security posture in place of the US pack's HIPAA text.
fhir_profileA UK FHIR profile family (e.g., FHIR UK Core) in place of the US pack's US Core.
payer_plan_vocabularyNHS provider, integrated care board, and private-sector equivalents in place of the US pack's Medicare / Medicaid / commercial / self-pay.
currency / locale / date_formatGBP · en-GB · DD/MM/YYYY
data_residency_requiredData-residency requirement expected to be set true per NHS Data Security and Protection Toolkit, unlike the US pack's "no residency requirement."

Field names above are the real fields on JurisdictionPack (see the pack spec). No pack for the United Kingdom exists in code today — this table illustrates the mechanism, not a shipped configuration.

Source note

Regulator and law named for reference, not verified in-country: data protection — the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) under the Data Protection Act 2018 and the UK General Data Protection Regulation; clinical coding — the NHS Digital Transformation Directorate and SNOMED CT UK edition; clinical safety — DCB0129 (safety and assurance framework) and DCB0160 (clinical safety validation).
