Australia

Planned — no date, no partner, no filing

Nothing on this page is a claim of regulatory approval, compliance, deployment, or a partner in Australia. It describes, at website depth, what a jurisdiction pack for Australia would need to carry — illustrated against the real fields the pack interface already defines — and states plainly what has not been validated.

What a pack for Australia would carry
Pack fieldWhat we'd expect, to be validated in-country
coding_systemICD-10-AM and ACHI in place of the US pack's ICD-10-CM/CPT.
privacy_regimeThe Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles in place of the US pack's HIPAA text — a named slot in the pack interface already (`PrivacyRegime`).

payer_plan_vocabularyMedicare (public) and private health insurers in place of the US pack's Medicare / Medicaid / commercial / self-pay.
currency / locale / date_formatAUD · en-AU · DD/MM/YYYY
data_residency_requiredData-residency posture to be confirmed in-country; not assumed either way.

Field names above are the real fields on JurisdictionPack (see the pack spec). No pack for Australia exists in code today — this table illustrates the mechanism, not a shipped configuration.

Why it's on the list

A mixed public/private system where private insurers pre-approve and adjudicate, English-language, and — per canon — receptive to US health-technology.

Source note

Regulator and law named for reference, not verified in-country: privacy — the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC), under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles; software-as-a-medical-device posture — the Therapeutic Goods Administration (TGA).
