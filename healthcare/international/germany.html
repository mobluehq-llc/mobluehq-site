Germany

Planned — no date, no partner, no filing

Nothing on this page is a claim of regulatory approval, compliance, deployment, or a partner in Germany. It describes, at website depth, what a jurisdiction pack for Germany would need to carry — illustrated against the real fields the pack interface already defines — and states plainly what has not been validated.

What a pack for Germany would carry
Pack fieldWhat we'd expect, to be validated in-country
coding_systemICD-10-GM (diagnoses) and OPS (procedures) in place of the US pack's ICD-10-CM/CPT.

currency / locale / date_formatEUR · de-DE · DD.MM.YYYY
data_residency_requiredData-residency requirement expected to be set true, unlike the US pack's “no residency requirement.”

Field names above are the real fields on JurisdictionPack (see the pack spec). No pack for Germany exists in code today — this table illustrates the mechanism, not a shipped configuration.

Why it's on the list

Largest EU health market, with statutory utilization review (Medizinischer Dienst) and the EU AI Act's high-risk logging and traceability obligations (from August 2026) named in canon as a driver for a receipt-shaped record.

Source note

Regulator and law named for reference, not verified in-country: data protection — the German federal and state data-protection authorities, under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Bundesdatenschutzgesetz (BDSG); diagnosis/procedure coding — the German Institute of Medical Documentation and Information (DIMDI's successor function, BfArM) publishes ICD-10-GM and OPS.
