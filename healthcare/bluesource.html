blueSource

Cryptographic eSource for clinical trials

Secures clinical trial data collection with cryptographic signing and tamper detection, producing verifiable electronic source records.

Module — in active build
Request a pilot conversation Read the manual
Runs locally · CLI or web UI · Ed25519-signed receipts · no cloud account required
Who it's for

Clinical trial sponsors, CROs, and research integrity teams.

What it is not

Not a trial management system, not an electronic data capture (EDC) platform on its own, and not a replacement for existing trial infrastructure.

Status, measured

Demo build (v1) complete with core cryptographic signing, receipt generation, and verification tools.

Not yet true: No clinical trial deployment. No real trial data processed.
