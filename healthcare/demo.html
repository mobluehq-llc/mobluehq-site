Demo

blueHealthcare runs in your web browser — the launcher with all eight products, on synthetic data. There is nothing to download or install. Nothing in the demo is real — no real patient, member or claimant appears anywhere in it.

Open the demo

Open blueHealthcare demo →

Opens demo.mobluehq.com in a new tab. Works in any current browser on a Mac, Windows PC or tablet.

Signing in

  1. Your browser first asks for a username and password for the demo site.
  2. The blueHealthcare sign-in page then asks for its own username and password.
  3. MOBLUEHQ sends you both, separately. Request access.

Inside the demo

  • The first page shows every blueHealthcare product. Choose one to open it.
  • Inside any product, the ← All products button at the top takes you back to that page.
  • The demo is switched on for scheduled sessions. If the page does not load, it is switched off — contact us to arrange a time.
