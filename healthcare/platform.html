The record beneath the overlay

Thirteen clinical modules compose a shared platform record — the foundation every overlay product runs on top of. This page describes the modules as they exist today.

What it is

A 13-module clinical platform that records care team interactions, observations, medications, orders, results, and service requests — with role-based access control, cryptographic audit logs, and FHIR compliance. The modules are:

  • core.record — the base record types (Patient, Observation, MedicationRequest, etc.) with FHIR mapping
  • core.terminology — coding system resolution (ICD, SNOMED, LOINC) plugged into the jurisdiction pack
  • build — the compiler that validates and assembles records at test time and at startup
  • bridge.ops — operational connectivity to external LIMS and records systems
  • admit — patient check-in and registration workflows
  • schedule — appointment and slot management
  • note — clinical note authoring and annotation
  • flow — order routing and task dispatch
  • result — lab and imaging result ingestion and display
  • order — lab and diagnostic order creation and tracking
  • meds — medication preparation, administration, and verification
  • accession — specimen accessioning and chain-of-custody
  • image — medical image storage and viewer integration
What it is not

It sits beneath the systems a clinic already runs, or serves as the record system where none exists — a shared foundation the blueHealthcare overlay products build on. Every decision and every clinical assertion belongs to a named human.

Patient access — in development

What exists: A patient-facing module that verifies a signed patient identity before serving anything — signature checking, tamper detection and expiry are implemented and tested. This is the machinery that proves a request belongs to the patient it claims; it is not yet a way for a patient to sign in, which is the piece below. A patient can read only their own record. Two endpoints work today: a record summary and the patient's allergy list.

What v1 will add: Read-only access to results, problems, allergies, immunisations, procedures, and encounters. Patients will reach this through a patient portal sign-in (identity proofing and enrollment not yet built).

What is not built: Appointment requests, bill payment, and patient sign-in. These remain planned and are not available in the demo.

Current state

Synthetic data only. No live PHI has been processed. The platform runs in local demo mode with seeded, fixture-based records.

Care-team-scoped access control: Role-based authorization is enforced across all record types. Care-team-scoped access — restricting a user to records for patients on their own team — is enforced for 5 of 15 record types as of commit ef3d1a5: Encounter, Observation, ServiceRequest, DiagnosticReport, MedicationRequest. The remaining 10 types receive care-team scoping but it is not enforced; they are inert gates pending full rollout.

Startup safety gate: The platform refuses to start outside a synthetic-fixtures posture while any record type remains unenforced, so partial enforcement cannot silently reach a production-shaped deployment.

No production deployment exists yet. Everything described above is the local demo state.

The platform records care team decisions, not outcomes. A named human remains the only authority for every clinical assertion.
