blueChart manual

Maps a coverage requirement to the exact chart evidence that supports, contradicts, or never addresses it — then hands a human medical director a signed packet, not a verdict.

What this product is and is not

Is: Utilization-management evidence mapper.

Is not: blueChart never approves, denies, recommends, or alters coverage. It is not a prior-authorization decision engine.

Who uses it

Medical directors and UM reviewers at a health plan or delegated UM vendor.

Getting started — the local demo

From the product's own repository:

make demo
make verify-demo

This runs entirely offline against synthetic fixtures and exits non-zero on any mismatch. It does not connect to any production data source.

What a receipt proves
A signed receipt proves what blueChart ran and recorded; it does not prove an underlying real-world claim is true. Anyone can check the signature offline with the standalone verifier shipped alongside the product, without installing blueChart itself.
Known gaps, stated plainly
  • No signed pilot yet.
  • No live PHI has been processed.
FAQ
Does blueChart make the decision for me?

No. blueChart never approves, denies, recommends, or alters coverage. It is not a prior-authorization decision engine.

Does blueChart replace the system I already use?

No — it is an overlay. Your existing record system, LIMS, or claims platform stays exactly where it is.
