NO DATA CENTERS

Pro mode doesn’t run in a data center. It runs on slumberNet — a mesh of members’ own computers helping each other while they’d otherwise sit idle. The world doesn’t need another power-hungry building for most AI work; it needs the machines we already own working together. Every Pro search you run is compute the grid already paid for — verified, receipted, and shared. Good for your ideas. Good for the planet.

QUICKSTART — FIVE MINUTES
Download & open. First launch may ask for two "Always Allow" keychain approvals — that's macOS protecting your sign-in, once.
Sign in with Google or email. Have a coupon? Redeem it under Account → Pro code for a free Pro week.
Pick a pool (the sources your search draws from) and choose Local or Pro.
Write your question. The harder and more specific, the better blueMonster hunts. One paragraph is plenty.
Press Search and walk away. The board fills live: ideas → screened → attacked → developed → Ready to Judge. Every column explains itself, even when it's empty.
Stop when you're ready. You get the MBR — Mine blueMonster Run — the report built for exactly that: mining everything the run learned.
NEW SEARCH — HOW IT WORKS
Pick a starting Pool. Choose which source material the search will hunt through — use a bundled starter pool or one you've built.
Write your Directive. Describe the problem space or question you want blueMonster to explore — not the solution. One clear paragraph works best.
Literature search terms (optional). Enter exact terms to query public sources like PubMed, arXiv, or news — one per line. Leave blank if you're working purely from your pool.
Choose Search depth, Duration, and type. Pick Local (on-device, private) or Pro (slumberNet mesh, receipted). Adjust depth and duration to match your timeline.
Hit Start and walk away. The board updates live as ideas move through the funnel: generated → screened → attacked → developed → judged.
GUIDES — QUICK READS
The MBR (“Mine blueMonster Run”) → your favorite AI

MBR stands for Mine blueMonster Run — and that's the instruction. It's the run's final report: winners, near-misses, the whole screened corpus, and provenance for every claim. It's built to be mined by an AI — instructions for the AI are embedded at the top of the file.

  1. Open your run → Export MBR (a single .md file).
  2. Attach or paste it into your assistant.
  3. Ask: "Follow the mining instructions at the top of this report."

Pools — download & upload

A pool is the source material a search hunts through. The demo ships with starter pools; grab more from the community or build your own.

  • Download: Pools → Browse → add to your library.
  • Upload / share: Pools → yours → Share — it exports a single file anyone can import.
  • Modify: Download a pool to your library, upload your own seed list, or share a pool file with a friend — pools are just files.
  • Pools never carry your search results — only sources.
Question lists — the engine's instincts

blueMonster hunts by asking questions of every idea: can this be simplified? combined with something cheaper? moved to another industry? Lists learn — questions that find winners rise, duds fade.

  • ⬇ share downloads a list as a file to give a friend.
  • Upload imports theirs — stats start fresh so it learns your patterns.
Local vs Pro vs Premium

Local: private, on-device, nothing leaves your Mac. Pro: the verified GPU mesh — bigger models, receipts on every step, independent judging. Premium (coming): frontier models only where they're provably worth it. Switch per-search; the board and MBR work the same everywhere.
