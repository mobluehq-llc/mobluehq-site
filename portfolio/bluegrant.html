blueGrant

OFFICIAL-SOURCE GRANT AND FUNDING SCOUTING · A MOBLUEHQ PRODUCT

Pursue opportunities you can win and still afford to deliver. blueGrant crawls official funding sources, scores each opportunity against the real cost of applying — not just the size of the award — and drafts a packet for you to review.

WHAT IT DOES

Crawling is restricted, by design, to a fixed allowlist of official sources — any .gov/.mil domain plus a short list of named funding platforms (grants.gov, challenge.gov, NSF, SBIR.gov, ACL.gov, XPRIZE, HeroX) — never blogs, newsletters, or open-web aggregators. A source outside that list is refused unless a human overrides it deliberately.

Each opportunity is scored on a burden-first, 100-point model: eligibility and the real cost of putting an application together weigh as much as the size of the award.

From the official rules and your own project details, blueGrant drafts an application packet — a starting point for a human to edit and finish, not a finished submission.

Nothing is ever submitted on your behalf. There is no submission path in this product today; drafting stops at a packet you review and file yourself.

A “Global” edition points the same scoring and drafting engine at funding opportunities outside the U.S. It is the same underlying build, not a separate product — jurisdiction-specific eligibility and disclosure rules for non-U.S. programs are not built out yet.

EVIDENCE AND REFUSALS
NO SUBMISSION PATH

blueGrant drafts a packet. It does not, and today cannot, file anything with a funder on your behalf.

UNCALIBRATED SCORE

The 100-point score is a stated heuristic, not a result checked against real award outcomes.

HUMAN-REVIEW-REQUIRED

A drafted packet needs a person's review and edits before it's ready to submit.

STATUS

blueGrant has not shipped a public build. It is not yet wired into a live blueMonster run as a mode inside the app — that integration is not built yet.

No win-rate, funding-secured, or accuracy numbers are published for this tool. “Request access” adds you to the list for when a pilot opens; it does not start a download or a purchase.
