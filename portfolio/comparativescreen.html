COMPARE CANDIDATES DIRECTLY · A MOBLUEHQ PRODUCT

Choose among candidates by comparing them directly, not trusting an unstable absolute score. comparativeScreen runs head-to-head comparisons against criteria you declare up front, and tells you when the comparisons disagree with each other instead of quietly picking a winner.

HOW IT'S ORGANIZED
Screenings → Criteria → Tournament → Rankings → Receipts
WHAT IT DOES

At least one comparison criterion has to be declared before anything can run. A comparison with no stated criterion is refused, not defaulted to some general notion of “better.”

Candidates are compared against each other — either every pair (round robin) or a bounded set of matchups for larger groups (Swiss-style) — instead of being scored one at a time in isolation.

If the comparisons contradict each other — A beats B, B beats C, C beats A — that cycle is detected and named. The candidates involved are reported as unresolved instead of being silently forced into an arbitrary order.

A ranking distinguishes a clear result from a call that came down to a tie-break, so you can see which placements are solid and which aren't.

Every comparison and the resulting ranking export as a receipt: a durable, independently inspectable record of what was compared and how it came out.

EVIDENCE AND REFUSALS
CANNOT_CONCLUDE

The comparisons formed a contradictory cycle (A over B, B over C, C over A); the candidates involved are reported as unresolved.

NO CRITERION DECLARED

A comparison can't start until at least one criterion is stated.

TIE-BREAK, NOT PROVEN

A ranked position that came from a tie-break is marked as such, distinct from a clear result.

STATUS

comparativeScreen has not shipped a public build, and does not yet call a live model to run comparisons — today it reads pre-produced verdicts rather than judging candidates itself end to end.

No accuracy, drift, or bias numbers are published for this tool, and none are claimed relative to absolute scoring. “Request access” adds you to the list for when a pilot opens; it does not start a download or a purchase.
