IF IT DIDN’T WORK

The first question after launch loads the model into memory. Later questions in the same session do not pay that cost again.

It never escalates my hard question by itself

That is the design, not a fault. blueGlu may suggest that a question would be better served by a larger model, but it does not send anything anywhere without you approving that specific question.

I cannot find the paid plan

There is not one. blueGlu has no paid tier and no billing surface today. Frontier escalation uses your own API key, billed by that vendor to you.
