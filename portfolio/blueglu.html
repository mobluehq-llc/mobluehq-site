HOW IT WORKS

🖥 Local by default

Your question is answered by a model that runs on your Mac — no cloud round trip is needed to get an answer.

⚡ Escalate manually

Want more? Click Escalate and blueGlu sends your question to a stronger model. This is a control you use — nothing escalates automatically.

REQUIREMENTS

blueGlu 0.1 requires macOS 14.0 or later on Apple silicon. That's not an arbitrary cutoff — the runtime bundled with the app needs it.

QUICKSTART
Download & open. blueGlu is free — no purchase, no account required to install.
Ask your question. blueGlu answers it using a model running locally on your Mac.
Need more? Click Escalate. Your question goes to a stronger model — only when you choose to send it.
GUIDES — QUICK READS
Local vs Escalate

Local: blueGlu answers using a model already running on your Mac — this is the default for every question. Escalate: a manual control you click when you want a stronger model to take a question — it is never triggered for you. Model choice, answer quality, and cost outcomes are not assured either way.

Free at 0.1

blueGlu 0.1 is free to download and use. There's no pricing or subscription on this page — if that changes, this page changes with it.
