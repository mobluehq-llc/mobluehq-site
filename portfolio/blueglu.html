GUIDES — QUICK READS
Citations, not guesses

Every answer blueGlu gives from your files is grounded in a specific passage. If nothing in your files supports an answer, blueGlu says so rather than answering from general knowledge without telling you.

Approving before anything leaves

The first time a question would use anything other than your on-device model, blueGlu shows a consent screen: where it's going, what will be sent, what won't, and any expected cost. Approve once for that message, or for the rest of the conversation — nothing goes out until you do.

AI can be wrong

blueGlu is AI and can make mistakes. Treat every answer, including cited ones, as something to double-check rather than something to trust outright — see the full disclaimer.
